Questions and Answers

The common questions asked and answered by the HavenRoots Foundation


Frequently Asked Questions

What is HavenRoots Youth & Community Foundation?

HavenRoots is a non-profit foundation founded in 2021 that empowers communities through ecological stewardship, youth skill-building, sustainable urban agriculture, and emergency food relief.

What are HavenRoots' core focus areas?

Our core pillars include environmental reforestation, youth mentorship and after-school tutoring, organic community food gardens, and grassroots storytelling.

Where is HavenRoots located?

Our main office and youth hub are located at 124 Hope Street, Central Community Precinct, Johannesburg, with community gardens and projects across local townships like Dobsonville and Soweto.

How does HavenRoots support youth development?

We run after-school tutoring programs, academic mentorship (such as mathematics support), digital skills training, and eco-entrepreneurship workshops for youth aged 6 to 18.

What is the goal of the Urban Reforestation program?

Our reforestation goal is to plant and maintain over 25,000 indigenous trees across urban heat corridors by 2028, reclaiming underutilized municipal lots into biodiverse eco-hubs.

How do the community vegetable gardens work?

We establish organic community vegetable gardens to address local food insecurity, training residents in permaculture and providing fresh produce to daily after-school meal programs and local families.

How can I volunteer with HavenRoots?

You can volunteer by signing up through our Enquiry page, choosing areas such as tutoring, urban gardening, meal preparation, event planning, or administrative support.

What are the volunteer operating hours?

Standard volunteering and office hours are Monday to Friday from 08:00 to 17:00, and Saturdays from 09:00 to 13:00 for youth programs and garden maintenance.

How can organizations partner with HavenRoots?

Organizations and corporations can partner with us by sponsoring youth programs, providing in-kind donations of equipment or supplies, or establishing corporate social responsibility initiatives.

Can I make financial donations to HavenRoots?

Yes! You can support us through our Donate buttons on the website, contributing towards weekly meals, educational materials, or garden infrastructure.

Do you accept in-kind donations?

Yes, we accept in-kind donations including gardening tools, seeds, irrigation equipment, books, stationery, and non-perishable food items.

How does the Voices of HavenRoots platform work?

Voices of HavenRoots is our story hub where we publish firsthand accounts, resilience stories, and impact updates from students, volunteers, and urban farmers.

How can I schedule a site visit or tour?

Visitors and donors are welcome during standard hours, but we request that you schedule program site tours or group visits in advance via email at info@havenroots.com.

How do I subscribe to the HavenRoots newsletter?

You can subscribe using the newsletter form on our Voices or Home pages by entering your email address to receive real impact stories and event updates.

Who are the founders of HavenRoots?

HavenRoots was founded in 2021 by a dedicated group of community leaders, with Sipho Ndlovu currently serving as Executive Director and Co-Founder.

How can I contact HavenRoots directly?

You can reach us by phone at +27 11 782 0122, email us at info@havenroots.com, or fill out the message form on our Contact Us page.
