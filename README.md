<h2>Website Goals:</h2><br>
To gain the trust of corporate contributors, increase monthly recurring individual donations, expedite volunteer onboarding and event sign-ups, and offer transparent reporting.<br><br>
<h2>Objectives:</h2>
•	Within the first three months of launch, secure more than fifty new recurring monthly donations.<br>
•	Every month, more than thirty volunteer applications are processed online.<br>
•	Keep your mobile user accessibility score over 95 and reach a page load speed of less than two seconds.<br>
Key Features and functionality:<br>
•	A multi-tier contribution site that integrates secure payment methods (PayFast, Stripe, and PayPal) for both one-time and recurring monthly payments.<br>
•	An interactive volunteer registration form with skill-matching dropdowns and approval for background checks<br>.
•	An RSVP management system and event calendar for community workshops and fundraising campaigns.<br>
•	For openness, impact reports, financial summaries, and annual audit PDFs are available for download.<br>
•	For programme hubs, an interactive Google Maps location locator and live social media feed are included.<br><br>
<h2>Timelines and Milestones:</h2>
•	Weeks 1–2: Gathering needs from stakeholders, creating a content plan, gathering stories, and gathering assets.<br>
•	Weeks 3–4: Database structure, copy sign-off, wireframing, and high-fidelity UI design mock-ups.<br>
•	Weeks 5–6: Cross-device responsiveness testing, form validation, contribution gateway integration, and frontend development.<br>
•	Week 7: Official platform launch, live payment gateway verification, accessibility testing, and quality control.<br><br>
