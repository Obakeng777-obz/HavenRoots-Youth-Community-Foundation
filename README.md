<h2>Project Overview:</h2>
<ul>
  <li><b>Name:</b> Haven Roots Youth & Community Foundation</li>
  <li><b>History:</b> Haven Roots began as a grassroots community feeding programme in 2021 and has since expanded into a recognised non-profit organisation that offers emergency food 
      relief packages, urban community garden projects, youth mentorship programmes, and after-school tutoring to underprivileged families.</li>
  <li><b>Mission Statement:</b> By offering wholesome meals, educational help, and sustainable life 
      skills in a secure, welcoming setting, we hope to empower youth and build stronger 
      communities.</li>
  <li><b>Vision Statement:</b> To create self-sufficient communities where each child has access to a 
      good education, a healthy diet, and the chance to flourish.</li>
  <li><b>Target Audience:</b> Local families, business sponsors, individual contributors, volunteers in 
      the community, and young participants (ages 6–18).</li>
</ul>

<h2>Website Goals:</h2><br>
To gain the trust of corporate contributors, increase monthly recurring individual donations, expedite volunteer onboarding and event sign-ups, and offer transparent reporting.<br><br>

<h2>Objectives:</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Within the first three months of launch, secure more than fifty new recurring monthly donations.</li>
  <li>Every month, more than thirty volunteer applications are processed online.</li>
  <li>Keep your mobile user accessibility score over 95 and reach a page load speed of less than two seconds.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Key Features and functionality:</h2>
<ul>
  <li>A multi-tier contribution site that integrates secure payment methods (PayFast, Stripe, and PayPal) for both one-time and recurring monthly payments.</li>
  <li>An interactive volunteer registration form with skill-matching dropdowns and approval for background checks</li>
  <li>An RSVP management system and event calendar for community workshops and fundraising campaigns.</li>
  <li>For openness, impact reports, financial summaries, and annual audit PDFs are available for download.</li>
  <li>For programme hubs, an interactive Google Maps location locator and live social media feed are included.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Timelines and Milestones:</h2>
<ul>
  <li><b>Weeks 1–2:</b> Gathering needs from stakeholders, creating a content plan, gathering stories, and gathering assets</li>
  <li><b>Weeks 3–4:</b> Database structure, copy sign-off, wireframing, and high-fidelity UI design mock-ups.</li>
  <li><b>Weeks 5–6:</b> Cross-device responsiveness testing, form validation, contribution gateway integration, and frontend development.</li>
  <li><b>Week 7:</b> Official platform launch, live payment gateway verification, accessibility testing, and quality control.</li>
</ul>
