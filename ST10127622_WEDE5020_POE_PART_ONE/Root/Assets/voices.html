Featured Story

From Seed to Harvest: How the Soweto Urban Garden Feeds 200 Families Weekly

Published: July 28, 2026 | Category: Community Gardens

What started as an unused plot of land behind our youth center has transformed into a thriving 1,500 m² organic farm. Lead Gardener Thabo Mokoena
walks us through how sustainable permaculture practices are not only providing fresh vegetables for our daily after-school meals but also supplying
local families with affordable produce crates.
