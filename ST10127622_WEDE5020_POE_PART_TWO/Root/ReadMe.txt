Project Overview:
Name: Haven Roots Youth & Community Foundation
History: Haven Roots began as a grassroots community feeding programme in 2021 and has since expanded into a recognised non-profit organisation that offers emergency food relief packages, urban community garden projects, youth mentorship programmes, and after-school tutoring to underprivileged families.
Mission Statement: By offering wholesome meals, educational help, and sustainable life skills in a secure, welcoming setting, we hope to empower youth and build stronger communities.
Vision Statement: To create self-sufficient communities where each child has access to a good education, a healthy diet, and the chance to flourish.
Target Audience: Local families, business sponsors, individual contributors, volunteers in the community, and young participants (ages 6–18).

Website Goals:
To gain the trust of corporate contributors, increase monthly recurring individual donations, expedite volunteer onboarding and event sign-ups, and offer transparent reporting.

Objectives:
Within the first three months of launch, secure more than fifty new recurring monthly donations.
Every month, more than thirty volunteer applications are processed online.
Keep your mobile user accessibility score over 95 and reach a page load speed of less than two seconds.
Key Features and functionality:
A multi-tier contribution site that integrates secure payment methods (PayFast, Stripe, and PayPal) for both one-time and recurring monthly payments.
An interactive volunteer registration form with skill-matching dropdowns and approval for background checks
An RSVP management system and event calendar for community workshops and fundraising campaigns.
For openness, impact reports, financial summaries, and annual audit PDFs are available for download.
For programme hubs, an interactive Google Maps location locator and live social media feed are included.

Timelines and Milestones:
Weeks 1–2: Gathering needs from stakeholders, creating a content plan, gathering stories, and gathering assets
Weeks 3–4: Database structure, copy sign-off, wireframing, and high-fidelity UI design mock-ups.
Weeks 5–6: Cross-device responsiveness testing, form validation, contribution gateway integration, and frontend development.
Week 7: Official platform launch, live payment gateway verification, accessibility testing, and quality control.
